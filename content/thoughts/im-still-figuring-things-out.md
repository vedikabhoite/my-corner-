---
title: "I'm still figuring things out"
date: "2026-08-01"
excerpt: "And maybe that's actually the point."
---

I used to think there was a version of "having it figured out" waiting for me somewhere down the line — a point where the plan would finally feel settled and I could stop asking questions.

I don't think that point exists. Or if it does, it keeps moving.

What's actually changed is that I've gotten better at being okay with the in-between. Not everything needs an answer right now. Some things just need time, or a few more tries, or someone else's perspective, or a long walk.

So this is me, still figuring it out, writing it down anyway.
