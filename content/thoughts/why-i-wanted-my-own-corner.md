---
title: "Why I wanted my own little corner of the internet"
date: "2026-08-12"
excerpt: "Maybe not everything needs to become a portfolio."
---

Somewhere along the way, every profile I made online started to feel like it was auditioning for something. LinkedIn wanted a headline. Instagram wanted a highlight reel. Even my resume wanted a "personal projects" section that proved I was interesting in a hireable way.

This isn't that.

I wanted one place that didn't need to convert into anything — no call to action, no "hire me," no metric to optimise. Just a spot to write down the things I'm thinking about, keep the things I like, and leave a trail of what these days actually looked like.

If it turns into a portfolio eventually, fine. But it didn't start as one, and I'd like to keep it that way for as long as I can.
